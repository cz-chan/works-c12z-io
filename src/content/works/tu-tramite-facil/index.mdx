At Tu Trámite Fácil, I started out working in Growth and eventually took on the role of CMO. The challenge was to grow a product that connects citizens with the public assistance programs available to them in Spain. The gap is real: everything related to administrative procedures and government leaves much to be desired in terms of user experience. The product cross-references the user’s personal situation with databases of open funding opportunities to identify what assistance they can apply for, how much they might receive, and how to manage the process.

I developed go-to-market strategies for B2C and B2B, identifying ICPs and use cases based on user interviews. These helped me understand not only why people signed up for the service, but also why they decided not to, and to translate those insights into changes to the product, positioning, and marketing.

I worked on the entire funnel—from awareness and acquisition to activation, retention, and monetization—combining social media, email, and content-led strategies. I designed experiments on website flows, pricing, and positioning, and instead of simply comparing variants, I used counterfactual approaches to understand which changes improved conversion and which reduced CAC. At the same time, I built dashboards tracking marketing KPIs and unit economics that provided visibility into acquisition, LTV, and business growth, and served as a foundation for discussions with investors.
