I joined Growth and ended up as CMO of Tu Trámite Fácil (TTF), a legaltech company that matches citizens’ profiles with available public assistance programs to tell them which ones they’re eligible for, how much they can receive, and how to apply for them—all in an industry with a terrible user experience.
I developed launch strategies for two markets: families at risk of social exclusion, particularly those receiving rental assistance (B2C), and small businesses looking to go digital (B2B).
Studying both markets allowed me to understand citizens, their concerns, and why they act the way they do, and to tailor products and upsells to each grant, thereby increasing the number of grants requested and awarded per customer—and, as a result, our profit.

By understanding the user, we designed a strategy centered on _Awareness - Acquisition - Activation - Retention - Monetization_ to educate users about what types of assistance are available, which ones they are eligible for, and to show them that TTF was the logical and reliable solution.

The most interesting challenges were:

- Building trust: We managed thousands of euros per family, and many came to us skeptical because of past bad experiences; talking with them, as we did with [Magnusem](/works/magnusem), was key.

- Building the growth system: We started with no channels or data, recruiting our first users from among family and friends. Based on their feedback, we figured out which messages built trust and scaled our efforts using social media, influencers, email, and CRM until we established the acquisition, retention, and monetization loops.

- Quasi-experiments and metrics: Since we did not achieve statistical significance for A/B testing, we turned to counterfactual quasi-experiments comparing clicks, completed forms, and scheduled calls.

Through all these experiments and lessons learned, we reduced the CAC by ~20% in less than a year and saved more than an hour per customer.
