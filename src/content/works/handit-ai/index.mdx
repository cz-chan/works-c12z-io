I joined Handit AI as Growth & Marketing Lead to build the customer acquisition channel from scratch. I designed and executed the content marketing strategy and cold and warm outreach campaigns, running them through the founders’ own LinkedIn profiles rather than a brand account: in an early-stage company, a founder’s message opens doors that an unknown company’s message cannot.

The system was based on a defined ICP. Using Apollo and LinkedIn Business, I identified accounts that matched that profile and moved them through a three-step funnel—meeting, pilot, client—working LinkedIn and email in parallel. Measuring each step separately made it possible to see where people were dropping off and adjust the message rather than expand the list. In less than two months, the conversion rate from cold contact to pilot increased by about 20%.

The channel was working, but not at the pace required by the runway. The answer wasn't to optimize the funnel, but to change the model: during my last month on the team, I moved on to defining a new go-to-market strategy for Handit AI, shifting from a pay-as-you-go SaaS model to an open-source model monetized through infrastructure (IaaS). That meant rethinking awareness, acquisition, and monetization from scratch, including a referral-driven growth loop.

I worked on that project side by side with the founders, and it mainly involved understanding unfamiliar territory: how an open-source community behaves and is governed, and how to monetize it without relinquishing the intellectual property that underpins the product. I analyzed competitors and potential partners, and from there we defined how to generate traffic and a market entry strategy consistent with a model in which the product is given away for free and revenue is generated from the infrastructure.
