I joined Handit AI, an AI-native startup specializing in agent monitoring and automatic improvement, as Growth & Marketing Lead to build our customer acquisition and brand awareness from the ground up by establishing outbound, content, and email channels.

The first challenge was to define our ICP with the founder: technical profiles at startups between seed and Series B. We found them through Apollo and LinkedIn and filtered them using automations in n8n to write personalized messages—which were then reviewed manually from the founders’ profiles—to avoid spam. Through this outreach, combined with inbound strategies on LinkedIn, Reddit, and Product Hunt, we improved the conversion rate to pilot programs by ~20%.

To measure this, I built the growth analytics system from scratch, connecting the APIs from Lovable, HubSpot (as a CRM), and GitHub, along with an AI-powered feedback system on n8n that sent us daily recommendations for content and metrics.

Although the channel was working, it wasn't keeping up with the pace required by the runway, so we were forced to change our business model: from B2B SaaS to open source monetized through infrastructure (IaaS). That forced me to rethink everything from scratch:

- how an open-source community works and how it is governed,
- how these projects grow, and
- how to monetize the infrastructure without relinquishing ownership of the product's intellectual property.

From there, we analyze competitors, seek out partners and integrations with other open-source projects, and define our market entry strategy, with a growth loop based on referrals.
