Primitivo Sánchez has been installing telecommunications infrastructure in the Vega Baja region for over 40 years. The company had built its reputation through word of mouth, but its customers no longer ask their neighbors—they search on Google and, increasingly, ask an AI. The challenge was to bring those 40 years into a digital environment without diluting the brand along the way.

I handled the project end to end: architecture, development, SEO, and analytics.

On the front end, I used Astro and React. For the back end, Node.js and Express with PostgreSQL via Prisma, ensuring that the form filled out by a visitor didn’t end up as a standalone email but was instead entered directly into the database the company used.

Building on that foundation, I developed the layer for customer management, personal data, communications, and billing. The key was to connect the existing system—Factusol—with modern tools like Google Workspace and Resend, so that the workflow would be automated and much of the client’s manual work would disappear.

In terms of visibility, I researched keywords with Ranki, analyzed competitors in the area, and defined an SEO strategy focused on Alicante, Murcia, and, more specifically, the Vega Baja region. I optimized content, metadata, and site structure to improve local search rankings. Offline, I also placed QR codes with tracked URLs in strategic locations with [qiip.me](https://qiip.me).

The goal wasn’t traffic for traffic’s sake, but rather to show up exactly where that service is searched for: antenna installation, network maintenance in homeowners’ associations, and video surveillance stations.

To measure the effectiveness of this SEO and AEO strategy, I set up an analytics layer using GA4, Search Console, and Looker Studio, so that both my client and I could see what was working and make decisions based on data, not intuition. All of it compliant with the GDPR via Cookiebot.

Development, product, SEO, and operations were inseparable. Every decision in one area affected the others. The result is a piece of digital infrastructure for a company that had spent decades building infrastructure offline.
