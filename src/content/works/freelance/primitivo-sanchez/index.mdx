Primitivo Sánchez (PS) has been installing telecommunications infrastructure in the Vega Baja region for more than 40 years. The company had built its reputation through word of mouth, but its customers no longer ask their neighbors—they search on Google and, increasingly, consult an AI. The challenge was to bring those four decades into a digital environment without diluting the brand along the way.

I managed the project from start to finish: architecture, development, SEO, and analytics. I redesigned the digital identity while retaining the logo and colors to modernize the brand without losing the recognition its customers already had.

On the front end, I used Astro for the pages—which were mostly static—and React for the interactive sections. For the back end, I used Node.js and Express with PostgreSQL via Prisma, ensuring that the form filled out by a visitor wouldn’t end up as a standalone email but would be entered directly into the database the company was already using.

Building on that foundation, I developed the layers for customer management, personal data, communications, and billing. The key—and the challenge—was to connect the existing system, Factusol, with modern tools such as Google Workspace and Resend, so that the workflow would be automated and much of the manual work for the customer would be eliminated.

In terms of visibility, I researched keywords using Ranki and Keyword Planner, analyzed the competition in the area, and developed an SEO strategy focused on Alicante, Murcia, and, more specifically, the Vega Baja region. I optimized the content, metadata, and website structure to improve rankings in local search results.

The goal wasn’t to generate traffic just for the sake of it, but to appear exactly where people were looking for that service: antenna installation, network maintenance in homeowners’ associations, and video surveillance systems.

For the offline world, I placed QR codes with tracking URLs in strategic locations and places where PS was already a provider to make it easier to contact them via [qiip.me](https://qiip.me).

To measure the effectiveness of this SEO and AEO strategy, I set up an analytics framework using GA4, Search Console, and Looker Studio, so that both my client and I could see what was working and make decisions based on data, not intuition. All of this was done in compliance with the GDPR through Cookiebot.
