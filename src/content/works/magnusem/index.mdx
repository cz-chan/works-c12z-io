Magnusem was a PropTech company that specialized in renting rooms to students in Alicante and Murcia.

My job involved creating and managing the business from the ground up, from product development and financial modeling to customer acquisition and the day-to-day management of three properties with more than 10 rooms.

This project was quite a challenge for several reasons:

- Developing the business model and finding the right balance among the margins of the four parties: investors, tenants, property owners, and myself.
- Improving my communication skills to align four parties with very different interests, which required a lot of one-on-one conversations.

And even though it took me almost a year to turn Magnusem into a professional venture, transforming it from a simple idea on paper and in code into a project with real users and real revenue, I decided to shut down the company because the profit margin for my investor and me was negligible, and we needed a significant amount of time and a very large volume of properties and tenants to achieve a profit margin that we both felt comfortable with.

It was a good experience in a complex and outdated market that requires a lot of time spent talking with clients, especially property owners and real estate agents.
