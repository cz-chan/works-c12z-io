Magnusem was a PropTech company focused on renting rooms to students in Alicante and Murcia. I built and ran the business from the ground up, handling everything from product development and customer acquisition to sales and the day-to-day operations of three properties with more than 10 rooms. Part of that work involved building the website we used to attract tenants and manage the rentals.

This experience led me to work directly with students and property owners, from tenant acquisition and the rental process to customer relations. At the same time, I developed financial models and business projections based on actual operating data—both our own and that of other companies in the industry—to establish the unit economics needed to seek external investment.

In the end, I decided to shut down the operation after determining, based on financial and operational data, that the available runway wasn’t sufficient to achieve profitability. It was the experience that taught me to tie product, sales, operations, and business metrics into a single decision: knowing when to stop.
